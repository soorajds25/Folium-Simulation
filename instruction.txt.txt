1. PROJECT PURPOSE & OVERVIEW
The purpose of this project is to develop a "Professional Streamlit-Based Shell 
Eco-Marathon Vehicle Strategy Simulator and Optimization Dashboard." This software 
is strictly intended to operate as a high-fidelity motorsport strategy engineering 
platform. Its primary objective is NOT to serve as an educational toy or simple 
physics animator, but rather to determine a mathematically proven, globally optimal 
driving strategy for any given race track while strictly adhering to all Shell 
Eco-Marathon official competition rules, motor/battery boundaries, and time limits.

2. CORE DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY
- Optimization-First: The platform must treat the race as a mathematical trajectory 
  optimization problem solved in the distance-domain (s-domain). Strategy generation 
  must be completely automated by an internal optimal control solver. Manual creation 
  of throttle or burst profiles by the user is forbidden as a method of strategy planning.
- Mathematical Determinism: Every output must be traceable to underlying physical 
  laws (aerodynamic drag, tire hysteresis, motor torque envelopes, and battery internal 
  resistances). Heuristics should only be utilized where physical validation models 
  exist to parameterize them.
- Modularity & Future-Proofing: The application must decouple the track data parsing, 
  vehicle physics modeling, numerical optimization, visualization, and telemetry 
  subsystems. The architecture must explicitly support low-latency, real-time telemetry 
  ingestion without structural modification.

3. TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT BOUNDS & STAGES
The final software system must execute its operational lifecycle across five stages:
  - Stage 1: Standardized Track S-Domain Representation Ingestion (CSV/GPX/Manual).
  - Stage 2: Vehicle Dynamics and Powertrain Parameterization (Fixed & Tunable).
  - Stage 3: Feasible Bounds Profiling (Velocity envelope computation under cornering 
             and current limits).
  - Stage 4: Trajectory Optimization Engine execution (iLQR / Dynamic Programming).
  - Stage 5: Analytical Dashboard Visualization and Driver Assistance Blueprinting.

4. CORE SOFTWARE CONSTRAINTS
- Strict compliance with Shell Eco-Marathon Rulebook parameters (e.g., maximum 60V DC 
  system design, maximum 1000Wh Lithium-based battery capacity, and precise 14.8 km 
  total race distance over 4 laps under a 35-minute maximum time window).
- Absolute adherence to traction boundaries. The strategy generator must never command 
  tractive or cornering forces that exceed physical track adhesion parameters.
- Execution environment: The core solver logic must execute cleanly within standard 
  numerical constraints, eliminating non-repeatable control chatter or humanly 
  impossible burst segments.
